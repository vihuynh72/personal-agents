# Agentic Gatherings

**Three-minute hackathon pitch · English speaker script**

## 0:00–0:25 · Slide 1 · The problem

*Stage cue: Open on the invitation.*

> You text five friends, “Dinner at my place this Saturday?” One can make it. One is vegan. One is still checking. Now you have to figure out who’s coming, what everyone can eat, and how much food to buy. And if someone drops out, you have to adjust the plan. You sent one text. Now you’re coordinating dinner.

## 0:25–0:45 · Slide 2 · The solution

*Stage cue: Introduce the solution.*

> We built Agentic Gatherings to handle that work. Tell your agent who you’re hosting, when dinner is, and what you want to spend. It works with your guests’ personal agents to plan the meal and prepare a shopping basket for you to review. Let me show you.

## 0:45–1:15 · Demo · Guest preferences

*Stage cue: Switch to the product. Submit the host request and show Maya’s response.*

> I’m hosting dinner for six this Saturday. My agent checks with my guests’ agents. Here’s Maya. She’s chosen to share two things for this dinner. She’s free on Saturday, and she needs a vegan option. She’ll still decide herself whether to accept the invitation.

## 1:15–1:55 · Demo · The menu changes

*Stage cue: Point to the ingredient evidence. Pause two seconds after the change.*

> One option is this creamy mushroom pasta. The ingredient list shows it contains milk. Now watch what happens to the menu.
>
> My agent picks a vegan option and shows me the ingredient source. I can see what changed and why. I review and approve the plan before the invitations go out.

## 1:55–2:35 · Demo · The basket is ready

*Stage cue: Show the confirmed headcount, then the basket. Point to quantity and total.*

> Once the guests reply, the agent uses the confirmed headcount to work out how much food to buy. Here’s the basket. I can see what it picked, how much we need, and what it costs. If I want to change something, I can. When I’m happy with it, I approve the basket.

**Optional, if verified:** “One guest can’t make it. Watch the quantities update as we go from six people to five.” If checkout is ready, briefly show it after approval. Label test mode and simulated delivery if used. Return to the last slide at 2:35.

## 2:35–3:00 · Slide 3 · Closing

*Stage cue: Look up from the screen.*

> Maya decided what to share. The guests decided whether to come. And the agent turned their replies into a meal plan and a basket I could approve.
>
> So the next time you text, “Dinner at my place?”, you can spend a little less time coordinating and a little more time looking forward to dinner.
>
> I’m Jing. Thank you.
